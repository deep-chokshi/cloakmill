# Community code of conduct

CloakMill welcomes contributors across backgrounds, experience levels, and disciplines.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing work.
- Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and intellectual property.
- Use fictional examples instead of exposing another person's or company's data.
- Make room for questions and constructive disagreement.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and moderation

Report concerns to the repository owner through an existing private channel. If none is available, request a private contact route without identifying affected people or publishing incident details. For concerns involving the owner, use GitHub's reporting tools.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or decline contributions to protect the community. Reports should be handled discreetly and fairly, without a promised response-time SLA.
